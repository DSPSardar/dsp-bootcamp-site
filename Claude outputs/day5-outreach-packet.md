### 10 questions (EN / UR)

1. Your age range? — آپ کی عمر؟
   18–22 / 23–28 / 29–35 / 36–45 / 46+
2. Your city? — آپ کا شہر؟
   Karachi / Lahore / Islamabad–Rawalpindi / Peshawar / Quetta / Other Punjab / Other Sindh / Other KP / Other / Abroad
3. What best describes you? — آپ کیا کرتے ہیں؟
   Student / Fresh graduate / Employed / Freelancer / Business owner / Not working right now
4. Have you used an AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) in the last 30 days? — پچھلے 30 دنوں میں AI ٹول استعمال کیا؟
   Daily / Weekly / Once or twice / Never
5. Can you write code? — کیا آپ کوڈ لکھ سکتے ہیں؟
   No / A little / Yes, comfortably
6. Have you ever paid for an AI course? — کبھی AI کورس کے لیے پیسے دیے؟
   No / Yes, under PKR 5,000 / Yes, PKR 5,000–20,000 / Yes, over PKR 20,000

   Get a job / Freelance for clients abroad / Sell to local businesses / Use in my own business / Just curious
