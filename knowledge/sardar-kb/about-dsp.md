## The company

Digital Services Program (DSP), legal name Digital Services Program Pvt. Ltd., is an AI agents training company and AI agency in Islamabad, Pakistan. It teaches beginners to build, deploy and sell AI agents in Urdu and English through DSP AI Agent Mastery, and builds AI Employees for businesses worldwide. It is not affiliated with any government digital-services programme of the same name (India, the EU and the UK have those).

- Founded: 2022. Part of the Sardar Group of Companies.
- Tagline: "We build AI agents for the world. We train the world to build them."
- Two divisions: DSP Agents builds AI Employees for businesses (Zara, Adam, Maya, Emma), all running on DSP Agent Hub, whose product line is ASOS, the AI Sales OS (dspagenthub.com). DSP Academy teaches people to build them, today through DSP AI Agent Mastery, a self-paced 16-module program. The spine is Learn → Build → Deploy.
- Languages: Urdu and English.
- Where students and clients come from: Pakistan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia and Malaysia.
- Students: 350 total enrolled in DSP AI Agent Mastery (DSP's ASOS student dashboard, as of 15 September 2026).
- History: DSP's live seven-day bootcamp, the DSP Agentic Lab (Vibe Coding Bootcamp), ran six batches with five live Zoom classes per batch and four certificates (three from Anthropic, one from DSP). It was sunset on 30 August 2026; the Mastery lectures were recorded in those cohorts, and Mastery is its self-paced successor. There are no more live batches. DSP also runs ChannelOps, a YouTube channel cleanup service and course.
