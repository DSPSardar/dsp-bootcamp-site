# ASOS — AI Sales OS, on DSP Agent Hub: what it is, who it's for, proof, how to get a demo

Source: the DSP public site (src/config/site.ts `agency`, /ai-employees, /pricing, the homepage Agent Hub section) and the ASOS product repository README. Product site: https://dspagenthub.com

## What it is

DSP Agent Hub is the AI sales platform every DSP AI Employee runs on, and the system DSP runs its own admissions on. Its product line is ASOS, the AI Sales OS. Tagline: "Close deals while you sleep."

ASOS is a WhatsApp-first AI Sales Operating System: a multi-tenant WhatsApp sales pipeline with an AI qualifier and closer agent pair handling inbound leads. The Qualifier AI reads each incoming message, scores the lead and classifies intent; the Closer AI writes the reply and drafts the follow-up sequence. Every lead, message and outcome is logged in the tenant's pipeline.

How a lead moves through it:
1. Lead: a message lands on the business's WhatsApp, at 11 PM, on a Sunday, mid-rush.
2. AI qualification: the AI Employee replies in seconds, answers from the business's price list, and finds out what the customer needs.
3. Conversation: a real back-and-forth, in English or Urdu, not a scripted menu.
4. CRM: every lead, message and outcome logged in the Agent Hub pipeline.
5. Follow-up: the second and third message a human team forgets, sent on schedule.
6. Conversion: payment guided and confirmed before a sale is ever marked won.
7. Human handoff: anything sensitive or unusual goes to the team with the full conversation attached.

## What's in the platform

- Modules in the live app: Today, Dashboard, Leads, Conversations, AI Insights, Ads, Analytics, Students, Reports, Automations.
- Lead pipeline with stages, hot-lead view and handoff queue.
- Payment confirmation: a sale is only marked won once the money is confirmed.
- Automations and follow-up sequences.
- Daily digest to the business owner each morning, and a weekly performance digest.
- Analytics, reports and AI insights (including sentiment analysis).
- Google Sheets sync for leads.
- Multi-tenant: each business is its own isolated tenant with its own price list, knowledge base and rules; data is never shared between clients.
- Plans: FREE, PRO and ENTERPRISE tenant plans on the platform. DSP's managed AI Employee packages (below) are priced separately in USD.
- Works with the business's existing WhatsApp number via the official WhatsApp Business API. Prices or menu change? The owner tells DSP, or updates it in the dashboard, and the Employee knows immediately.

## The AI Employees that run on it

- Zara, AI Sales Employee (WhatsApp): answers every lead in seconds, 24/7, answers questions from the price list, qualifies, runs follow-up sequences until they decide, guides them through payment and only marks a sale won once the money is confirmed. Best for any business selling services or products over WhatsApp: coaching, real estate, retail, agencies. Zara is DSP's own admissions agent: messaging DSP's WhatsApp is the live demo.
- Adam, AI Support Employee (WhatsApp): answers the same fifty questions instantly, 24/7, in English or Urdu, from the business's knowledge base, and hands anything sensitive to the team with the full conversation attached. Best for businesses drowning in repetitive queries.
- Maya, AI Booking Employee (WhatsApp): takes booking requests around the clock, checks availability, confirms appointments and sends reminders so no-shows go down. Best for salons, clinics, car services, consultants.
- Emma, AI Order-Taking Employee (phone): answers a restaurant's phone on the first ring, takes the complete order with sizes and modifiers, confirms the total and texts the customer a confirmation. Best for restaurants, takeaways and cafés in the US, UK and Australia. Emma's pricing is quoted per business by call volume.

How a build goes: discover (DSP maps the services, price list, FAQs and rules), build (the job description and knowledge base are loaded into DSP Agent Hub and tested against DSP's 10-point acceptance sheet), live (the owner tests it on their own number, approves, and it starts answering). Live in 7 days.
