# Data Compliance Standards (plain-English, non-legal)
Practical standards for handling user data in a small client-facing agent. Not legal advice — for anything regulated (health, finance, children's data), get real legal guidance.

## Minimum standards for any agent built in this course
1. **Collect the minimum** — only ask for data the agent actually needs to do its job.
2. **Tell users what's stored** — a simple line like "we keep your name and order history to serve you faster" is enough for most small-business use cases.
3. **Never store sensitive data unnecessarily** — card numbers, passwords, ID numbers should never sit in your agent's memory or logs.
4. **Offer deletion on request** — even a manual process ("email us to remove your data") is better than nothing.
5. **Secure what you do store** — use the hosting platform's built-in database security rather than a plain text file with customer info in it.

## Why this matters even for a small project
Client trust is the actual business asset here — a café that finds out its AI Employee mishandled customer phone numbers will not renew. Good data habits are also good client-retention habits.
