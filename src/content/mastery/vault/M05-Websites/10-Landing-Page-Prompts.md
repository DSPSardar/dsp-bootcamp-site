# 10 Landing Page Prompt Templates
Copy-paste starting points for Claude Code. Fill in your own business details.

1. "Build a hero section for [business] with a headline that says [what you offer], a short subheading, and one clear call-to-action button that says [action]."
2. "Add a 'who this is for' section with 3-4 short cards, each naming a type of customer and what they get."
3. "Add a menu/services section listing [items] with name, short description, and price, in a clean grid."
4. "Add an 'about' section: 2-3 sentences about [the business/person], written in a [tone] voice."
5. "Add a contact/order form with fields: [list fields]. Show a clear success message after submit."
6. "Add a testimonials section with 3 placeholder quotes I can replace with real ones later."
7. "Add an FAQ section, collapsible, with these questions: [list]."
8. "Make the whole page fully responsive — check it looks right on a phone-width screen."
9. "Add a sticky header with the business name and a call-to-action button that stays visible while scrolling."
10. "Polish the visual design: better spacing, a consistent font pairing, and a colour palette based on [colour/mood]."

## Tips
- Do these roughly in order — structure first, polish last.
- After each one, actually look at it (don't just trust it worked) before asking for the next.
