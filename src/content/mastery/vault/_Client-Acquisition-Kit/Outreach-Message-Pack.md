# Outreach Message Pack
Keep every message under 80 words with one clear ask. Adapt the bracketed parts.

## WhatsApp — cold outreach to a local business
"Hi [name], I noticed [specific observation about their business — e.g. 'you're taking orders manually over WhatsApp']. I build simple AI assistants that handle exactly that — takes orders, answers menu questions, confirms automatically. I've got one running as a demo. Worth a quick look? Takes 2 minutes: [link]"

## LinkedIn — cold outreach
"Hi [name] — I build AI assistants for small businesses that handle bookings/orders/FAQs automatically, so you're not doing it manually. I have a live demo for a [their industry] business that's similar to what you might need. Would a 10-minute call make sense this week?"

## Email — cold outreach
Subject: "Quick idea for [business name]'s [ordering/booking] process"
"Hi [name], I help businesses like yours automate the repetitive parts of customer conversations — taking orders, answering common questions, booking appointments — using AI. I built a working demo you can try in 2 minutes: [link]. If it looks useful, happy to talk about what it'd take for [business name]. — [your name]"

## Follow-up (no response after outreach)
"Hi [name], just following up in case this got buried — happy to send the 2-minute demo again, or if now's not the right time, no worries at all."

## After a discovery call
"Thanks for the chat today, [name]. Putting together a short proposal based on what we discussed — I'll have it to you by [day]. One quick question in the meantime: [any clarifying question]."
