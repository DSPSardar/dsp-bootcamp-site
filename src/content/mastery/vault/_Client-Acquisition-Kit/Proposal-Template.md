Six sections, in this order. Keep it to 1-2 pages — clients don't read long proposals.

---

**[Business Name] — AI Employee Proposal**
Prepared by: [Your name] · Date: [date]

[1-2 sentences restating their actual pain, in their words from discovery — this shows you listened]

## 2. The Solution
[1-2 sentences describing the AI Employee you'll build — what it does, in plain language, no jargon]

- [specific capability 1]
- [specific capability 2]
- [specific capability 3]

- [anything out of scope for this phase]

## 4. Timeline
- Week 1: [milestone]
- Week 2: [milestone]
- Delivery: [date]

## 5. Price
[Total price], [payment terms — e.g. 50% upfront, 50% on delivery]

## 6. Next Step
[The one clear action — "reply to confirm and I'll send the agreement" or similar]
