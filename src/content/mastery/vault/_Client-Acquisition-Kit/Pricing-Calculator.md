# Pricing Calculator
A simple framework for pricing an AI Employee project — not a rigid formula, a way to think it through.

## Step 1 — Base build price
Estimate your hours for: discovery + JD writing + build + testing + deployment.
Multiply by your hourly rate (or use a flat project rate you're comfortable with as you're starting out — many freelancers in this course start around $300-800 for a first single-tenant build).

## Step 2 — Add for complexity
- +$: multiple integrations (calendar + email + sheets, not just one)
- +$: custom knowledge base requiring real content work
- +$: multi-tenant / multi-location from day one

## Step 3 — Ongoing costs (separate from the build price)
- Hosting: usually near-free at small scale (Vercel free tier, small Railway cost)
- API/token cost: typically $2-15/month depending on volume — quote this honestly, it's a real client cost
- Your support/maintenance: decide if this is included for a period (e.g. "30 days free support included") or a separate monthly retainer

## Step 4 — The retainer decision
Many freelancers under-price by treating this as a one-time sale. Consider offering: build price + a modest monthly retainer for updates, monitoring, and small changes — this is where recurring revenue actually comes from in this business model.

## A rough starting reference (adjust to your market and experience)
| Project size | Build price range |
|---|---|
| Simple single-agent, one integration | $300-600 |
| Standard AI Employee (your course project's scope) | $600-1,200 |
| Multi-tenant / multiple integrations | $1,200-2,500+ |
| Monthly retainer (updates + monitoring) | $50-200/month |
