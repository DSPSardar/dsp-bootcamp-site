# Getting Paid — Guide for Freelancers (Pakistan-based, international clients)
Practical options for receiving payment from clients, especially cross-border.

## For local (Pakistan) clients
- Bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa — the standard, low-friction options
- Always get at least partial payment upfront before starting work

## For international clients
- **Payoneer** — widely used by Pakistani freelancers, receives from many international clients and platforms, withdraws to local bank
- **Wise** — good for receiving international bank transfers with reasonable exchange rates
- **Direct bank wire** — works but often has higher fees and slower processing than the above

## What to avoid
- Accepting only a promise of payment after full delivery on a first-time client relationship — always get something upfront
- Informal cash arrangements with no written agreement (see the SOW/Contract template) for anything beyond a very small project

## The habit that protects you
Never start real build work without at least a simple written SOW (even a short one) and an upfront payment — this protects both your time and the professionalism of the relationship.
