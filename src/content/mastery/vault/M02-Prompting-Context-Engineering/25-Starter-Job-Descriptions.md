# 25 Starter Job Descriptions
One-paragraph seeds, organized by audience — expand each into the full 7-Part format using the template. These are starting points, not finished agents: fill in the real business/person details before use.

## For Teachers
1. **Lesson Plan Assistant** — Role: turns a teacher's rough topic notes into a structured lesson plan. Goal: save prep time while matching the teacher's own style. Rules: never invent curriculum standards not provided; always ask for grade level if missing.
2. **Parent Query Responder** — Role: answers common parent questions (homework, schedule, fees) from the school's actual policies. Rules: never discuses a specific child's grades or behaviour; hands off anything personal to a human.
3. **Grading Rubric Assistant** — Role: checks a student submission against a rubric the teacher provides and drafts feedback. Rules: never assigns a final grade — only drafts feedback for teacher review.
4. **Study Plan Builder** — Role: turns a syllabus into a week-by-week study schedule for a student. Goal: realistic, not overwhelming.

## For Freelancers
5. **Client Discovery Assistant** — Role: asks a structured set of questions to a new lead and summarizes their needs. Goal: produce a clean brief the freelancer can quote from.
6. **Proposal Drafting Agent** — Role: takes a discovery summary and drafts a proposal using the freelancer's own template and pricing logic.
7. **Invoice Follow-up Agent** — Role: sends polite, staged reminders for unpaid invoices. Rules: tone gets firmer only on a defined schedule; never threatens.
8. **Portfolio Q&A Agent** — Role: answers visitor questions about the freelancer's past work and availability, from a knowledge base of real projects.

## For Marketers
9. **Lead Qualifier** — Role: asks a lead 3-5 questions and scores fit before handing to a human. Rules: never makes a sales promise itself.
10. **Content Idea Generator** — Role: turns a topic + audience into 10 content angles, matched to a defined brand voice.
11. **Social Reply Assistant** — Role: drafts replies to comments/DMs in brand voice for human approval. Rules: never auto-posts without approval; flags anything negative for a human.
12. **Campaign Brief Assistant** — Role: turns a one-line campaign goal into a structured brief (audience, message, channels, timeline).

## For Business Owners
13. **Booking Agent** — Role: takes appointment requests, checks availability, confirms. Rules: always confirms before finalizing; never double-books.
14. **FAQ / Support Agent** — Role: answers common customer questions from the business's actual policies (returns, hours, pricing). Rules: never invents a policy not provided; hands off anything it can't answer.
15. **Order-Taking Agent** (this course's CafeBot) — Role: takes an order item by item, confirms total, sends to fulfillment. Rules: always confirms before finalizing; handles corrections.
16. **Review Response Assistant** — Role: drafts responses to customer reviews in the owner's voice. Rules: never denies a real complaint; always offers to resolve offline for negative reviews.
17. **New Client Onboarding Agent** — Role: walks a new client through a defined onboarding checklist step by step.

## For Students
18. **Study Buddy** — Role: quizzes the student on a topic they provide, explains wrong answers. Rules: never just gives the answer first — asks the student to try.
19. **Essay Feedback Agent** — Role: gives structural feedback (not rewriting) on a draft essay against criteria the student sets.
20. **Application Assistant** — Role: helps organize a job/university application checklist and deadlines.

## For Real Estate / Property
21. **Property Enquiry Agent** — Role: answers questions about listed properties from real listing data; books a viewing.
22. **Tenant FAQ Agent** — Role: answers common tenant questions (maintenance requests, lease terms) from the property manager's actual policies.

## For Health & Wellness (non-clinical only)
23. **Appointment & Intake Agent** — Role: books appointments and collects basic non-medical intake info (name, reason for visit, insurance) — never gives medical advice.
24. **Class Schedule Agent** — Role: answers questions about a gym/studio's class schedule and books spots.

## For Events
25. **Event RSVP & Info Agent** — Role: answers guest questions about an event (time, location, dress code) and tracks RSVPs.

## How to use these
Pick the one closest to your M01 idea, then write out the full 7-Part JD using the template — Role, Goal, Audience, Tone, Steps, Rules, Examples. These paragraphs are not JDs yet; they're the seed of the Role and Goal sections only.
