# Client Discovery Sheet
Use this in a real (or role-played) conversation with a prospective client before proposing anything.

**Business name:**
**Contact person & role:**
**Date:**

## The pain
1. What's the task you're currently doing manually that's taking too much time?
2. How often does this happen — daily, weekly, per customer?
3. Who currently does this, and what else could they be doing with that time?

## The current process
4. Walk me through exactly how this works today, step by step.
5. What tools do you currently use for this (WhatsApp, spreadsheets, a specific software)?
6. What goes wrong most often in the current process?

## The volume
7. Roughly how many of these happen per day/week/month?
8. Is this seasonal or steady year-round?

## Success looks like
9. If this were solved perfectly, what would change for you or your team?
10. Is there a number you'd use to measure success (time saved, more bookings, fewer mistakes)?

## Constraints
11. What's your rough budget range for solving this? (if they won't say, ask "would $X feel reasonable")
12. What's your timeline — is there urgency, or is this exploratory?
13. Who else needs to approve this decision?
