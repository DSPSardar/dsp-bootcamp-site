# Simple SOW / Contract Template
A lightweight Statement of Work for a small AI Employee project. For larger projects, have a lawyer review a fuller contract — this is a starting point, not legal advice.

---

**Statement of Work**
Between: [Your name/business] ("Developer") and [Client business name] ("Client")
Date: [date]

**1. Scope of Work**
Developer will build: [restate scope from the Proposal — be specific]

**2. Timeline**
Estimated delivery: [date]. Delays caused by late client feedback/content may extend this timeline.

**3. Price & Payment**
Total: [amount]. Payment terms: [e.g. 50% to begin, 50% on delivery]. Payment method: [specify].

**4. What's Included / Excluded**
Included: [list]
Not included (available as additional work at [rate/quote]): [list]

**5. Revisions**
[e.g. "Up to 2 rounds of revisions on the agreed scope are included. Additional changes billed at $X/hour."]

**6. Ownership**
On final payment, Client owns the deployed AI Employee and its code. Developer retains the right to use the underlying template/architecture for other clients.

**7. Support**
[e.g. "30 days of bug-fix support included from delivery date. Ongoing support available as a separate monthly retainer."]

**8. Cancellation**
[e.g. "Either party may cancel with written notice. Client pays for work completed to date."]
