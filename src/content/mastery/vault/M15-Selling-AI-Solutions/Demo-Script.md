# Demo Script — showing your AI Employee to a prospect
A simple structure for a live demo call (or even a recorded video demo).

## 1. Frame it (30 seconds)
"This is an AI Employee I built — it's the same kind of system I'd build for [their business]. I'll show you exactly how a customer would use it."

## 2. Show the happy path (1 minute)
Walk through a normal interaction live — a customer asking a question, placing an order/booking, getting confirmation. Narrate what's happening in the background (checking the menu, confirming the total) without over-explaining the tech.

## 3. Show one edge case handled well (1 minute)
Show a correction ("actually change my order") or a question it answers accurately from real data — this proves it's not just a scripted demo.

## 4. Connect it to their situation (1-2 minutes)
"For your business, this would instead [specific adaptation — their menu/services, their booking flow, their notification preference]."

## 5. The ask
"Does this look like something worth exploring for [business name]? If so, I'd love to ask a few questions about how you currently handle this so I can put together something specific to you."
