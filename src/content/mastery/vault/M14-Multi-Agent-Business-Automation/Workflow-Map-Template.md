# Workflow Map Template
Map out the full flow — not just the conversation, but everything that happens around it.

```
[Trigger: how does a conversation start? WhatsApp message / website chat / etc.]
        ↓
[Agent conversation: greet → gather info → confirm]
        ↓
[Tool calls: what gets checked or updated — menu, calendar, database]
        ↓
[Confirmation back to the user]
        ↓
[Automation: what happens after — notification sent? log updated? reminder scheduled?]
        ↓
[Any human follow-up needed? When and how?]
```

Fill this out for your own AI Employee before the M14 assignment — it forces you to see the whole business process, not just the chat window.
